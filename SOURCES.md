# Sources

Everything in this repository was made by us, with the tools below, or is the bundled three.js library. Nothing is
downloaded artwork.

## Code

- `reef.js`, `life.js`, `extras.js`, `bottom.js`, `index.html`, `tools/`: ours, MIT (see `LICENSE`).
- Caustics, light shafts, jellyfish, the anemone, marine snow and plankton: our own shader and geometry code, MIT.
- `vendor/`: three.js r187 (npm `three@0.187.0`) with its GLTFLoader, BufferGeometryUtils, SkeletonUtils and meshopt
  decoder, unchanged. MIT, © 2010-2026 three.js authors; see `vendor/LICENSE-three.txt`.

## The reef backdrop (`scene/`)

- `bg.webp`: an image we generated with **FLUX 3 Image Klein** (Black Forest Labs, open weights) in ComfyUI, from our
  own prompt. That model's licence (FLUX Kommunity License v1.0) governs use of the model; Black Forest Labs claims no
  ownership of its outputs. **AI-generated.**
- `bg_depth.bin`, `bg_normal.webp`, `scene.json`: depth and surface normals of that image estimated with **MoGe-2**
  (ViT-L, normal variant; weights MIT), then baked into a terrain the animals can see.

## The animals (`models/*.glb`)

Each model was made in three steps:

1. a reference photo of the species on a white background, generated by us with FLUX 3 Image Klein (as above);
2. **Tripo** image-to-3D on a paid plan, under which the user holds the rights to the outputs, including to
   distribute and license them (Tripo Terms, section 5.2.2);
3. our own clean-up: head turned to +x, scaled to length 1, simplified, textures to WebP, meshopt compression
   (glTF-Transform).

**AI-generated.**
